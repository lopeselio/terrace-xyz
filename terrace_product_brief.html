Salient features

Permissionless markets

🏆
Parlays
Chain multiple markets into single conviction plays. Your multi carries your badge.

Matchups

Flash contests
Short, live, high-urgency markets during matches. Half-time calls, next-goal scorer.

Leverage
Amplify conviction on markets you're sure of. Borrowed exposure, not borrowed bets.

Frontend tooling
Embed Kalshi & Polymarket liquidity. Creators earn from their own market activity.
