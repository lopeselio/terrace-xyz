01 · What's new

Only what fans will feel

Eleven things shipped between Wed 14 and Mon 19 October that a fan can see and use. The ops, error-handling and AI-fallback work stays off social. Each feature gets a code used throughout this calendar.

📰

Audio

Pick by mood, then check it's cleared

  • Streaks (R2, R5, P2): a ticking build that drops when the calendar fills. Search the TikTok Commercial Music Library for clock, countdown or suspense.
  • Feed and match rooms (R1, R7): high-energy football-edit beats. Phonk and drum & bass are the house sounds of football TikTok. Search phonk, drum and bass or stadium.
  • Reactions and comments (R3, R4): comedy timing. Use TikTok's own sound effects (crowd "ooh", record scratch, a boom on the 🤡) over a quiet bed.
  • Founder (R6): your voice first, with a lo-fi bed at 5–10%.
  • Our own: the ad's Mixkit #190 track fits the posters. Check its licence before using it in anything you boost.

Finding what's trending (2 minutes, on the day)

  • TikTok: Creative Center → Trends → Songs, set the region to United Kingdom and turn on Approved for business use. Pick something rising, not already peaked.
  • Instagram: in the Reels audio picker, look for the ↗ arrow (trending). Business accounts only see cleared tracks.
  • Volume: trending sound at 5–15% under any voice-over, or as the only audio for no-voice Reels.
  • Post the silent export and add the sound in-app. A sound added in-app counts for discovery; one baked into the file doesn't.

I can't see live charts from here, so pick the exact track on the day using the steps above.
