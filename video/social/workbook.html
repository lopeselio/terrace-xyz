01 · Strategy

The strategy on one page

Terrace turns the argument in your group chat into a game. So the content should feel like football banter first and like a product second. Show the take, the mates, the full-time whistle and the "called it" moment, then show the app.

Goal 1
Sign-ups

Fans who open play.terrace.social and make a passkey account. This is the number that matters.

Goal 2
Builder cred

Monad, Chainlink, Envio, Alchemy and Mera communities see Terrace as the reference consumer app. This feeds judging and partnerships.

Goal 3
Takes

Fans post takes on the app. Every take is a market, and every market is the next post.

Voice

  • Talk like a fan in a group chat, not a fintech. Use "back it", "fade it", "called it", "your mates".
  • On TikTok and IG, put no crypto words in the first line. Monad and Chainlink come later, as trust signals.
  • On X, the tech is the story, so name the sponsors.
  • Stay short and sure of yourself. Not hypey. One emoji at most per post.

Every post needs

  • A hook in the first second: on-screen text, a face or a goal.
  • One idea. One feature, one take or one result.
  • A next step: comment your take, follow, or try it at play.terrace.social.
  • The testnet / free-to-play line wherever money appears on screen (see Rules).

04 · Cadence and timing

How often, and when

Football runs on a weekly clock: matchday on the weekend, the Champions League midweek, quiet days in between. Post to that rhythm, not a flat schedule.

TikTok
1–2 / day

Volume wins. Two a day on matchdays, one on other days. Reply to comments with videos.

X
3–5 / day

1 video or thread, 2–3 single tweets, and 10+ replies in Monad and football threads. Live-tweet matchdays.

Instagram
4 Reels + 2 carousels / wk

Cross-post the best TikToks (without the watermark). Stories daily on matchdays.

07 · TikTok playbook

TikTok: how we win there

TikTok shows your video to strangers first, so the first second decides everything. Lead with a football feeling (a face, a goal, a cheeky line) and let the app show up as the punchline.

Hook rules

  • Big text on screen in frame 1, kept inside the safe zone (away from the right rail and bottom caption)
  • Start mid-action, never on a logo
  • Use POV, "my mate…" and "tell me you…" framings

Edit rules

  • 7–25 s. Loopable endings do well.
  • Trending sound at 5–15% under the voice, or as the only audio
  • Captions always on
  • Cut every 1.5–3 s

Growth moves

  • Reply to comments with a video ("Your take is now a market")
  • Stitch big football takes from creators
  • Post 3–5 hashtags, not 30
  • Comment on football creators' videos within an hour of them posting

11 · Rules and guardrails

Stay out of trouble

Money on screen

  • Terrace runs on testnet with test money. Say "free to play" or "test money" in the caption of any post that shows dollar amounts. The ad already carries "Illustration. Testnet: no real money."
  • Never say "win money", "earn", "profit" or "guaranteed". Talk about points, bragging rights and calling it.
  • Don't promise future rewards for points.

Platform policy

  • TikTok and Instagram restrict gambling and financial-product promotion, and paid ads are much stricter than organic posts. Before boosting any post, read the current ad policy and lead with the social game.
  • Don't pay creators without a clear #ad disclosure.
  • Under-18s: never target or feature them.

Music

  • The ad and demo bed use Mixkit track #190. Check its licence before using it in posts, and don't use it in any paid boost without checking.
  • For reach on TikTok and Reels, post the silent cut and add a sound from the in-app library. Business accounts can only use the commercial library.

Football IP and people

  • Club crests, broadcast footage and match clips are copyrighted. Use our own app footage only.
  • Player names in a take are fine. Don't use player photos.
  • Don't show a real fan's name or chat without their OK.
  • Don't imply the clubs, leagues or sponsors endorse Terrace. "Built with Chainlink" is fine; "partnered with" isn't, unless that's true.
