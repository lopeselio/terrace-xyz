06 · Checks

Before each post

  • Money: "Free to play · test money" wherever money comes up. It's already burned into the vertical clips. Never "win money", "earn" or "guaranteed".
  • Hashtags: no #betting, #gambling, #crypto, #nft or #airdrop on TikTok or Instagram. Keep crypto words to X.
  • Sponsors: "Built with Chainlink" and "on Monad" are fine. "Partnered with" isn't, unless it's true.
  • Ahmed is the pitch's example fan. Don't present him as a real user's quote.
  • Tracking: tag links per post, e.g. play.terrace.social/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=PT1, and log the numbers in the workbook's tracker 48 hours after posting.
