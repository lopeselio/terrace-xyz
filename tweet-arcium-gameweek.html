Terrace × Arcium MPC
Sealed end-to-end
Picks are ciphertext on Solana. Scoring is an MPC function, not a server.
TuePick lock
Pick encrypted client-side via x25519 + RescueCipher to the MXE pubkey. Sealed on-chain as Enc<Mxe, u64>. Terrace can't read it. Helius can't. Your league mate can't.
Sat 12:30Kickoff
Validators, RPCs and indexers see 32 bytes of opaque ciphertext per pick. No insider edge — there's no plaintext to leak.
Sun FTMPC scoring
Oracle posts public winning_outcome. Arx node committee runs the circuit over secret shares — if pick == winning { score += 1 }. Both branches execute; no single node ever reconstructs pick. New score returns as Enc<Mxe, u64>.
Reveal
You tap .reveal() at GW end — plaintext score lands on-chain via #[arcium_callback]. Your pick stays sealed forever unless you opt-in.
Cryptographic guarantee. The MPC circuit IS the scoring rulebook — same .arcis binary, verifiable, deployed on Solana. No backend to trust.
Across a 38-week PL season, information asymmetry compounds — one insider with DB access can quietly copy the leader's picks for months. Sealed picks + MPC scoring kill that vector. The function below is compute over ciphertext, not just storage.
