Matchups

Settle the group chat.
Real money. One winner.

1

Alice sends the challenge

She picks "Will Liverpool win the UCL?", picks YES, enters Bob's wallet, and stakes $5. Her money locks in immediately. Challenge sent.

2

Bob accepts and takes the other side

Bob sees the challenge in his app. He automatically gets NO. No negotiation needed. He puts in his $5. The pot is now $10.

3

The match plays out

Liverpool win or they don't. The result is confirmed and recorded automatically. No manual input needed. No arguments about who said what.

4

The winner gets the full pot

Either player taps Settle. The app checks who called it right and sends the full $10 straight to their wallet. No fees. No waiting. Instant.

The rules

⚔️ Both sides always stake the same amount
✓ Winner takes double their stake
✓ If Bob never accepts, Alice gets her money back
✓ The app settles it. No arguments. No middleman.

Why this matters

Group chats are full of big talk. Matchups let you back it with real money against a named opponent. Win and your rep goes up. No bookmaker. No house. Just you versus your mate.

⚔️

1v1. Equal stakes.

Pure conviction.
No house edge. No spread. No excuses.

Why Solana

Take out the tech.
The app stops working.

Without it

  • Your reputation lives in a company database. They can edit it, sell it, or delete it tomorrow.
  • Anyone who wants to create a market has to ask permission and give the platform a cut.
  • Winnings sit in a company account. They decide if and when you get paid.
  • The whole thing collapses if the company shuts down.

With Solana

  • Your reputation is stored in a place no company owns. It's yours forever. Anyone can verify it.
  • Any fan creates a market in 30 seconds with no permission from anyone. Earnings are automatic.
  • Winnings go straight to your wallet the moment the market settles. No waiting. No company in the middle.
  • The code runs independently. Even if we shut down, your record stays.
Transactions confirm in under a second
A $1 bet costs less than a cent in fees
