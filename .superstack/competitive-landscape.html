Substitutes (same fan, different approach)

Substitute
Bet365 / DraftKings / William Hill
Traditional regulated sportsbooks. Massive football coverage, live betting, fiat ease. Users stay because of trust, regulation, UX polish. Why they'd switch: Crypto users want self-custody + USDC yield on idle stake.
Substitute
Sorare
Fantasy football NFT trading with licensed player cards. Long-term squad-building engagement. Users stay for gamified weekly leagues and licensed IP. Different engagement model — weekly fantasy vs. match-by-match prediction.
Substitute
Kalshi / Robinhood Predictions
CFTC-regulated event contracts. Kalshi has 51% of US prediction volume; Robinhood processed 2.5B trades in Oct 2025. Users stay for regulatory clarity and fiat ease. Limited football depth.
2026 Wildcard
ADI Predictstreet (FIFA Official Partner)
FIFA's official prediction market partner for World Cup 2026. DAZN embedding their markets in live World Cup streams. Could capture massive casual football audience. Watch closely — if they go multi-chain or mobile-first, this becomes high threat.

Dead & Failed Projects — Lessons

Dead
Augur (Ethereum, 2015–2022)
6 years of development, never got consumer traction. Gas fees made small bets ($5–$50) economically unviable. Resolution disputes with no clear arbitration. Lesson: Low fees and clear settlement mechanics are table stakes — Solana solves both.
Pivoted / Dead
SportX v1 (Ethereum)
P2P sports betting exchange on Ethereum. Died from gas fees and two-sided liquidity bootstrapping. Rebranded as SX Bet with limited volume. Lesson: Bootstrapping a two-sided betting market is the hardest problem. Binary AMM markets (like Hedgehog) solve cold-start better than P2P matching.
Dead (External)
FTX Prediction Markets & Serum CLOB
FTX collapse wiped institutional Solana infrastructure in Nov 2022. Left a vacuum that's only now refilling. Lesson: Non-custodial, self-settling programs are the only viable architecture on Solana.
Never Gained Traction
Gnosis Conditional Tokens Apps
Powerful infrastructure with zero consumer layer built. Developer tools without product UX. Lesson: Infrastructure without a polished consumer surface dies. Terrace must be app-first, not protocol-first.

Moat Analysis

Primary Moat: Football Fan Community
Football fans are tribal by club, competitive by season. A community of bettors who share results, build win streaks, and flex badges creates network effects no fork can capture. "Terrace" as a brand is deeply resonant with football culture — standing section, collective passion, local identity.
Secondary Moat: Solana Mobile Stack Distribution
150,000+ Seeker phones shipped Aug 2025. A first-class dApp in the Seeker app store gets hardware-level distribution that no web competitor can match. The Solana Mobile Stack licensing to other Android manufacturers expands this further. No other football prediction market has this vector.
World Cup 2026 Timing
The largest single prediction market event in football history. DAZN embedding blockchain predictions in World Cup streams; FIFA has an official partner. First-mover in Solana mobile football markets before June 2026 captures a permanent brand association with the tournament. Wait = 4 years.
Biggest Threat to Moat
Hedgehog Markets ships a React Native app + football-specific UI. They have $20M TVL and 3,500 active users — the hardest parts of bootstrapping. Estimated 3–6 month replication window after Terrace shows traction. Execute on mobile distribution and community before they notice.

Recommended Differentiation — The Terrace Wedge

Polymarket is for political nerds. Hedgehog is for DeFi traders. Terrace is for the fan who argues about Arsenal's xG at the pub and wants skin in the game.

1
Football-first, fan-culture design. Every UX decision should be optimised for someone who watches football, not someone who trades DeFi. Match cards > abstract market listings. Club colours, match timers, league tables. Never show "basis points" or "AMM fee".
2
Seeker as the launch distribution channel. Submit to the Seeker dApp store on Day 1. 150K potential users with no equivalent competitor. Frame the launch as "the prediction market built for the Seeker."
3
Simple binary USDC markets (Phase 1) → parlays (Phase 2). Start with the simplest bet — "Will X win?" — to eliminate friction. Parlays (multi-leg Parlay PDA) are the killer feature for fan engagement and will separate Terrace from generic prediction markets.
4
Reputation PDA as social graph. Win streaks, badge tiers, and public prediction history create the social proof layer missing from every competitor. The person with a "12-match correct streak" badge drives word-of-mouth in a way TVL numbers never can.
5
Prioritise oracle settlement reliability before launch. Creator-authority settlement is a centralisation risk. Validate a trusted oracle path for match results (Pyth, UMA, or a curated oracle multisig) before the World Cup markets go live. One settlement dispute in June 2026 will destroy trust permanently.
6
Consider Azuro as a data source, not a competitor. Azuro's multi-chain infrastructure (football-dominant, $358M volume) could be an oracle or liquidity bridge rather than a competitor. Integrating Azuro odds as a settlement reference would dramatically increase settlement confidence on Solana.
